How to Play

  1. Drag the three pieces onto the 8×8 board, in any order.
  2. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Several at once score much more.
  3. Clear on consecutive moves to build a combo, up to ×3.
  4. ◆ Pulse Blocks clear the 8 cells around them when their line clears. A blast that hits another Pulse sets it off too.
  5. ⟳ Rotate a piece with its button. Each turn uses 1 Rotation Energy; each line you clear restores 1, up to 3.
  6. The game ends when none of your pieces fit, even with the rotations you can afford.
