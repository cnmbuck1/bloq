            How to Play

            1. Drag the three pieces onto the 8×8 board, in any order.
            2. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Several at once score much more.
            3. Clear on consecutive moves to build a combo, up to ×3.
            4. ◆ Pulse Blocks clear the 8 cells around them when their line clears. A blast that hits another Pulse sets it off too.
            5. ⟳ Rotate a piece with its button. Each turn uses 1 Rotation Energy; each line you clear restores 1, up to 3.
            6. Level up as your score grows. Each level refills your Rotation Energy, brings tougher pieces and, every two levels, a new block material.
            7. ✓ Combo Protection lets an active combo survive one placement that clears nothing. Clear again to restore it.
            8. 2× Overdrive charges as you clear lines, fire Pulses and empty the board. When it reads READY, tap it: your next five placements score double and rotations are free (no energy used). The gold board frame and the 2× POINTS chip count down the placements left.
            9. Perfect Clear: empty the whole board in one move for a 2,000 point bonus.
            10. Daily Challenge: everyone gets the same board and pieces each day. You have 30 placements to score as much as you can.
            11. The game ends when none of your pieces fit, even with the rotations you can afford. A charged Overdrive keeps it going if its free rotations would open a move.
